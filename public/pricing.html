FAQ

Quick answers

The questions merchants ask before integrating.

How do I get started?
Pick one of two routes:
  1. Self-hosted. Run npm install -g @zettapay/listener, point it at your public key and start it on your own infrastructure. The walkthrough is in /docs/quickstart.
  2. Cloud. Create an account at /app with an e-mail, a shop name and your public key (a BIP-84 xpub for Bitcoin; an xpub or a fixed receive address for Base); a webhook URL is optional. Your API key is shown once — paste it to sign in to the dashboard. Cloud is in early access, so features may change.

Either way you only ever hand over public material. No wallet connection, no private key.

If there’s no transaction fee, how does ZettaPay make money?
From the Cloud subscription. ZettaPay is never in the flow of funds — the payment goes straight from the payer to your wallet — so there is no way for us to take a cut, and we don’t want one. What costs us money is running the listener for you, so that is what the flat monthly plan pays for.

If you would rather not pay anything, self-host: the listener is open source under MIT, free forever and has no limits.

What counts as an invoice, and what happens at the cap?
Plans count the invoices you create in a calendar month: 50 on Free, 500 on Starter, 5,000 on Pro.

When you reach the cap, creating new invoices is paused until the next month begins or until you upgrade. Invoices already created are not affected — they keep being watched, confirmed and delivered to your webhook.

Need more than 5,000 a month? Contact us.

How do I pay for a plan or upgrade?
Sign in to your dashboard at /app and pick a plan. There are two ways to pay:
  • In crypto. You get a normal ZettaPay invoice. Once it confirms, the plan is active for 30 days; to renew, pay again.
  • By card. A subscription that renews monthly. The card page is hosted by Stripe — ZettaPay never sees card data.

A paid plan that is not renewed falls back to Free. There is still no per-transaction fee. These are early-access prices and may change.

Do you run identity checks?
No. ZettaPay is identity-free. Self-hosting needs no account at all. Cloud asks for an e-mail, a shop name, your public key or receive address and a webhook URL — no identity documents and no wallet connection.

ZettaPay is non-custodial: funds move directly from the payer to you on-chain and we never hold them. You remain responsible for your own legal and tax obligations where you operate.
